Mall Sales and Customer Analytics — 2025

A holistic data analytics project involving analysis of sales in malls, customer behavior, performance of products sold, profitability, payment methods, membership categories, and sales channels using Python and Excel.


2.Purpose

The project involves analysis of **sales in 8,000 transactions in malls in 2025** to gain insights regarding sales performance, customer purchasing behavior, performance of products sold, and profitability.

The project includes **exploratory analysis using Python** and an **Excel dashboard** to gain insights from transaction level data.

Short Description / Goal

The goal of the project is to analyze the transaction data in the mall and answer the following business questions:

- What product categories make up the highest sales?
- What months show the highest and lowest performance?
- What customer age ranges have the highest number of orders and sales?
- What role do returning customers play?
- What membership categories make more sales?
- What payment methods are used?
- What is the difference between online and offline sales?
- What product categories are making profit?

5.Business Problem

Raw transaction data is quite informative; however, it does not offer a quick insight into the business performance.

The major objectives of the analysis include:

- Sales trends identification
- Profitability analysis
- Performance analysis of products and categories
- Customer behavior analysis
- Analysis of repeat customers' performance
- Performance analysis of memberships
- Preferences of payment methods
- Sales channels performance analysis

The main aim is to transform the transactional data into insightful analytics.

Customer Analysis

Analysis of age groups, gender, membership, repeat customers gives a lot of information about various customer segments.

Sales Planning

Monthly trends in sales give an idea about which months are better and which are weaker.

Profitability Analysis

Profit analysis along with sales gives a more holistic picture of business performance.

Payment Analysis

Sales Channel Analysis
